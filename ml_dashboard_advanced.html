🏆 Ensemble Strategy

Combines all models using R² weighted averaging:

  • Each model's weight = its R² score (0-1)
  • Higher R² = better model = higher weight in ensemble
  • Result: Smoother forecasts, reduced individual model noise
  • Ensemble typically outperforms individual models
