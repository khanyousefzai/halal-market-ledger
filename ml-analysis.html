Method. 3 years of daily closes from Yahoo Finance (split/dividend adjusted). At 20 past dates, 10 trading days apart, every model is fit on the previous 500 trading days only, forecasts the next 5, and is compared with what actually happened. "Backtest error" is the average absolute % gap over those 5 days. XGBoost predicts daily returns from the last 10 returns. The ensemble is a plain average of the three models. Forecast dates skip weekends and NYSE holidays.

Not investment advice. Point forecasts only (no confidence ranges). A past edge, or lack of one, can change in a different market. Download results (JSON)
